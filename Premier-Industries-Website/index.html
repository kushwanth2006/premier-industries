Our Story

Built on precision.
Grown on trust.

Founded in 1995 as a small-scale partnership with zinc electroplating lines, Premier Industries has evolved into a premier surface treatment company serving the country's largest automotive OEMs. We upgraded to a fully automated zinc plating line in October 2020.

  • 95
    1995
    Established as a partnership unit with zinc electroplating lines at Thirumazhisai SIDCO.
  • 98
    August 1998
    Became sole supplier to Mando Automotive India Pvt. Ltd. for Caliper and Carrier zinc plating — a relationship that continues to this day.
  • 20
    October 2020
    Upgraded to a fully automated zinc plating line supported by Grauer & Weil (India) Ltd., achieving zero breakdown performance.
